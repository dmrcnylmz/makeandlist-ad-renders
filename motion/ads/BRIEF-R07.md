# R07 — 16 cinematic product films (cut-out products in 3D light, narrated)

Read `BRIEF-R02.md` … `BRIEF-R06.md` first; every house rule stands. R07's genre is the **premium product film**: the
look of a high-end brand launch — dark or ivory studio sets, light sweeps, soft reflections, slow camera moves, depth of
field, choreographed product motion — telling MakeAndList's story through the products a seller can make and sell.
Narration by Marian (`meta.vo`, `node vo.mjs gNN`) in at least 10 of the 16; the rest are music-led films.

## New cut-out products (`K.pub('r07/<id>.webp')`, transparent WebP, gpt-image-2, keyed)

k01-mug ("slow mornings") · k02-framed-print (protea, terracotta sun) · k03-tee (sunset) · k04-hoodie (navy, mountains) ·
k05-tote (monstera) · k06-notebook (lemons) · k07-sticker-sheet (woodland) · k08-greeting-card (bird) ·
k09-kids-book ("Forest Friends") · k10-calendar · k11-parcel · k12-poster-tube · k13-wedding-planner · k14-gift-tag.

They are concept products: tag scenes they lead with `K.demoTag('Concept products · AI-generated', x, y)`. Build sets
around them with CSS (gradients, floor reflections via a flipped faded copy, contact shadows, light sweeps, haze,
particles, bokeh), CSS 3D (perspective, translateZ, rotateY turntables, orbit cameras) and depth of field (blur by depth).
The r04/r05 photos and real app screens are available where a proof is needed.

## The 16 films

| id | file | title | film idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| G01 | g01-turntable | The Turntable | the mug turns on a lit turntable; light sweeps; "made once, printed per order" |
| G02 | g02-weightless | Weightless | the catalogue floats in zero-g, drifting on slow orbits — "a full catalogue, no stock" |
| G03 | g03-light-sweep | The Light Sweep | a single beam sweeps a dark set and reveals each product as it passes |
| G04 | g04-shadow-words | Shadow Words | products on a ledge cast shadows that form the words of the message |
| G05 | g05-ballet | Product Ballet | products move in choreographed formation on the beat, ending in the ML monogram shape |
| G06 | g06-colour-story | Colour Story | one design; the set floods with each colour as the tee/hoodie/mug change colour |
| G07 | g07-the-drop | The Drop | luxury-drop teaser: black set, spotlight, the collection is unveiled one by one (no fake scarcity) |
| G08 | g08-catalogue-tumble | The Catalogue | products tumble in slow motion onto shelves that build themselves |
| G09 | g09-macro-wide | Macro to Wide | extreme macro of the print texture pulls back to the product and its listing |
| G10 | g10-paper-theatre | Paper Theatre | a layered paper-cut stage (2.5D parallax) plays the flow: idea → files → store → print → parcel |
| G11 | g11-three-rooms | Three Rooms | one camera move through three dioramas: the seller's desk, the partner's print room, the buyer's home |
| G12 | g12-gift-guide | The Gift Guide | an editorial gift-guide spread assembles itself with the cut-outs; no prices, "made to order" |
| G13 | g13-seasons | Seasons on a Carousel | a rotating stage brings seasonal sets around (gift tag, calendar, kids book, greeting card) |
| G14 | g14-parcels | A Day of Parcels | parcels and tubes stack up as sample orders are approved (labelled sample); "each one paid from your partner account" |
| G15 | g15-monogram | The Monogram | brand film: the MakeAndList monogram builds from products; narrated manifesto |
| G16 | g16-flagship | The Flagship Film | the definitive narrated story of MakeAndList in 15 s, cinematic, with one real-app proof |

Verify with stills only (never render on the Mac); one stills run at a time.
