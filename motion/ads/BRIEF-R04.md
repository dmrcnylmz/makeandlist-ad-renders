# R04 — 16 ads that sell MakeAndList: what it is, how it works, what you gain

Same kit and house rules as `BRIEF-R02.md` and `BRIEF-R03.md` (read both: safe zones, honesty, demo tags, the lifted
13.0 s end card, cue names, pure function of time, both formats laid out on purpose, full meta). R02 and R03 were judged
"very good"; R04 must be **original** — new formats, new looks, new products — and must **sell**.

## The improved creative brief (the owner's prompt, sharpened)

> Make 15-second ads that make a small seller stop scrolling, understand MakeAndList in one watch, and want to try it.
> Each ad answers one buyer question — *What is it? Is it for me? What do I get? How does it work? What does it cost?
> Can I trust it? Can I sell physical products too?* — with one idea, one hook, one proof and one CTA.
> Speak to real sellers: teachers and parents who make printables, illustrators, Etsy-style digital-product sellers (never
> name Etsy), small boutique owners on ikas / WooCommerce / Wix / Shopware, people starting a side business.
> Lead with the gain, not the feature: **an idea becomes a finished product** (files, listing copy, images, video) **in
> your own stores**, **printed products without stock** through your own print partner, **you see the cost first**,
> **nothing goes live without your yes**, **one place for every store**. Show it with beautiful products (new images,
> not the old packs), real app moments where they prove the point, and craft that looks like a top studio made it.
> No invented results: no revenue, sales counts, rankings, reviews or testimonials.

## What the seller gains (use these, one per ad)

1. Time: from a sentence to a finished product — the app plans, makes, checks and writes the listing.
2. No design skills needed: describe it; review real files before anything is sold.
3. Your stores, your customers: listings go to the seller's own stores; one place for all of them.
4. Physical products without stock: a print partner prints each order; the seller pays the partner from their own account.
5. Know the cost first: a quote in Credits before anything is made; unused Credits come back if it stops.
6. Control: drafts first; nothing goes live and no print order is sent without the seller's yes.
7. Quality: pages counted, sizes and print sharpness checked before a listing.
